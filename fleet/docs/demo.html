▶ Verbs (each wraps one doctrine)

pinrun

Runs every tools/pin_*.py in the lane, writes .pin-last, and the prompt dot flips. Pins are the fleet's fail-first proof law — the dot is that law, worn visibly.

walref

Prints the last wal_ref in receipts/*.jsonl. Fleet law: a claim about a run must cite its wal_ref or it is a rumor.

ship "msg"

Commit + push with a receipt-citing message, printing the short hash. The one-liner that keeps the ledger honest.

prqueue

Open PRs across the org — the Casey gate, visible from the shell.

lanes / recent

Jump to the lane root; heartbeat of the last commit per lane. Fleet awareness without leaving the terminal.

receipts theme

Prompt segments: lane name, pin dot, path, branch. Degrades gracefully outside a lane — plain shell elsewhere.

▶ Why this matters (the receipts-culture pitch)

The fleet's bet: effects are cheap, evidence is expensive. Most shells help you do things; this one tells you whether what you did is proven. The green dot is not decoration — it is the last pin verdict, following you around the tree. Break a pin and your prompt turns red before you even think about pushing. That is the ah-ha: the shell itself became a receipt surface.
